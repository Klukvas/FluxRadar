Simple prose

The cat sat on the mat. The dog ran to the park. Kids play in the sun. Birds sing in the trees. The sky is blue and clear. We eat lunch at noon. Mom reads a book. Dad cooks a meal. The day is warm and nice. We go for a walk. The park has a big pond. Ducks swim near the shore. A boy throws a ball. His dog runs to get it. We sit on a bench. The wind feels cool and soft. Soon the sun goes down. We walk back home. Dad turns on the lights. We eat a snack and rest.
